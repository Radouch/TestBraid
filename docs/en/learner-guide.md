# Learner guide

**English** · [Čeština](../cs/learner-guide.md)

TestBraid lets you complete published multimedia tests **without registering or signing in**.

## Open a test

Choose a lesson and test from the home page, or follow a link shared by an author. Only published tests are available to learners.

The website interface may offer different languages. Changing the interface language does not translate the questions themselves.

## Answer questions

All questions appear on **one scrollable page**. Use the question overview to jump between questions, and expand or collapse question cards as needed. Your answers are preserved while navigating the page.

Tests may contain text, images and playable videos.

### Choose answers

For a **single-choice** question, select one option. For **multiple-choice**, select all the options you think are correct.

### Match pairs

Press **Select** on an item in the first group, then **Assign** on its counterpart in the second group. The finished pair moves into **Your pairs**. Use **Unpair** if you want to change it. On phones the groups may appear above and below one another.

You can review and change your choices until you submit the test.

## Check your answers

Select **Check answers** when ready. The test shows correct, incorrect and unanswered questions. For a wrong matching pair, it also shows the correct counterpart.

A question counts as correctly answered only when it is fully correct. Some tests hide the **overall result** (the number of correctly answered questions), but still show feedback on individual answers.

Use **Try again** to start a new attempt. Your previous choices are cleared; matching items may appear in a different order.

## Privacy

Answers are evaluated in your browser and **are not stored as learner results**. TestBraid does not track your progress or send your answers to the author. Leaving or reloading the page may discard an unfinished attempt.
