# Why TestBraid

**English** · [Čeština](../cs/why-testbraid.md)

TestBraid is a **simple tool for making tests**. You build tests from text, pictures and videos, publish them on your own website, and anyone with the link can take them and see right away which answers were correct. That is all it does, on purpose.

## Why it exists

Many online form and quiz tools limit where your content can come from. For example, Google Forms can show videos only from YouTube. If your material is your own video, a recording you are not allowed to publish on a video platform, or simply a picture on your computer, such tools get in the way.

TestBraid has no such limits. Every picture and video in a test is a file **you upload** to your own hosting (JPEG, PNG or WebP pictures, MP4 or WebM videos). Nothing has to be published on YouTube or any other service first. Your tests and files stay on your hosting, under your control.

## What it is good for

- **Self-check quizzes with pictures and videos:** recognising plants, birds, traffic signs, musical instruments, sign language, sports moves, and anything else that is easier to show than to describe.
- **Teachers, trainers, clubs and parents** who want to share practice tests with a link, without any setup for the people taking them.
- **Learners who must not be tracked.** Learners do not register or sign in. Their answers are checked in their own browser and are never sent to the server or to you.
- **Ordinary web hosting.** TestBraid runs on common Apache/PHP hosting without a database. Your data is a set of files that you can back up by copying them.

## What it is not designed for

- **Exams or graded tests.** A published test contains its correct answers, so a determined learner can find them. Use TestBraid for practice and self-assessment only.
- **Collecting results.** Nobody's answers or scores are saved. TestBraid does not record who took a test or how they did.
- **Learner accounts and progress.** There are no learner accounts, classes, assignments, deadlines or progress reports.
- **Teams of administrators.** The site has author accounts only, and they all have the same rights. The first one is created in the browser; any further accounts can only be added by someone with SSH access to the server. There are no roles, permissions or account management screens.
- **Other kinds of questions.** Questions are either choice questions (one or more correct answers) or matching pairs. There are no written answers, timers or partial points. Text is plain text without formatting or links.

## It is not an LMS

A learning management system (LMS), such as Moodle, manages courses, students, grades and progress. TestBraid does none of that and never aims to. If you need those things, use an LMS.

TestBraid is deliberately small: one site, a few authors, tests made of text, pictures and videos, and nothing to manage for the people who take them. If that is what you need, start with the [Quick start](quick-start.md).
